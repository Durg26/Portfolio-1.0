The Impact Awards recognize students and staff at Dalhousie who have made meaningful contributions to campus life. It is the kind of event where the details matter because the people being honoured have earned something real.

## My role

I managed end-to-end production for the 2025 ceremony. That included coordinating venue and catering, writing communications to nominees and guests, building the run-of-show, and being on-site to manage the day.

## What made it different

Awards ceremonies can feel hollow if you are not careful. The format is predictable: speeches, applause, photographs. The challenge is to make the people being recognized actually feel recognized, not just processed through a program.

The changes we made were small. Longer descriptions for each recipient. An opening that named what the awards were actually for. A room arrangement that let nominees sit with the people who nominated them.

None of these were large. Together they shifted the energy of the room.

## On producing events that matter

The thing I think about most from this event is the gap between what gets measured and what matters. We can count attendees and photograph the stage. We cannot easily measure whether someone felt genuinely seen.

That gap is where the work is.
